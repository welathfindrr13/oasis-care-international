# Oasis Care Competitive Switch Strategy (Undercut + Profit)

Date: 2026-03-02
Status: Execution-ready strategy for winning agency switches

## 1) Market Reality: Who You Are Competing Against

For UK home-care software, the high-visibility competitors to track are:
1. Birdie.
2. CareLineLive.
3. Nourish.
4. PASS by everyLIFE.
5. Access Care Planning.

These are credible "top group" incumbents in this segment, but there is no single official ranking list.

## 2) Competitor Baseline (What Buyers Already See)

What agencies are told today:
1. End-to-end operational stack (care planning, eMAR, rostering, reporting).
2. Compliance and audit readiness.
3. Carer mobile workflows and alerts.
4. Family portals and integrations.

Public anchors we can use in sales:
1. CareLineLive: pricing starts at 120/month and per-carer monthly pricing is published.
2. Birdie: pricing starts at 200/month.
3. PASS/everyLIFE and Birdie list broad all-in-one capability on G-Cloud.
4. Nourish emphasizes eMAR scheduling, alerts, and audit trails.

Implication:
- You cannot win by claiming "we also do care planning and eMAR."
- You win by being materially better on economics, speed, service, and practical reliability.

## 3) Where You Can Beat Them (Switch Wedges)

## Wedge A: Better economics for small and mid agencies

Position:
1. "Enterprise-grade workflows without enterprise pricing."
2. Transparent package + overage model, no surprise module charges.

Your advantage:
1. Launch at 99/month undercuts common entry points.
2. Predictable per-org pricing can feel safer than pure per-carer billing.
3. Upsell path remains clear (Launch -> Starter -> Growth -> Scale).

Guardrail:
- Keep hard limits and overages to protect gross margin.

## Wedge B: Faster time-to-live

Position:
1. "Go live in days, not months."

How to make it true:
1. Standard migration templates (clients, carers, visits, meds).
2. Fixed onboarding playbook and cutover checklist.
3. Dedicated onboarding owner for first 30 days.

Buyer promise:
1. First agency branch live in <= 7 business days.
2. Full organization live in <= 21 business days.

## Wedge C: Better switch experience (service, not just software)

Position:
1. "We do the migration with you."

How to make it true:
1. Data import concierge.
2. Parallel-run support for 2 weeks.
3. Staff training sessions included.
4. Named CSM during onboarding.

Switch offers:
1. 90-day exit clause.
2. Migration fee waived for multi-branch deals.
3. Contract buyout credit (capped) for qualified opportunities.

## Wedge D: Practical reliability transparency

Position:
1. "If we say green, it is genuinely green."

How to make it true:
1. Publish readiness evidence from automated probes.
2. Show role-matrix pass rates and P1/P2 status.
3. Share incident communication standards and rollback policy.

This becomes a trust differentiator versus glossy demo-only pitches.

## Wedge E: Safer AI in real care workflows

Position:
1. "Actionable weekly AI summary, but privacy-first."

How to make it true:
1. AI summary from structured care events.
2. No direct identifier leakage in prompt inputs.
3. Admin approvals and audit trail for generated outputs.

You are not selling "generic AI"; you are selling "controlled clinical workflow acceleration."

## 4) Where You Should Not Compete Head-On (Yet)

Avoid claiming leadership today in:
1. Deep enterprise integration estate (especially where incumbents are mature).
2. National footprint/procurement scale credentials.
3. Broad marketplace ecosystem breadth.

Counter-position instead:
1. Better value + faster execution + more responsive product team.
2. Cleaner customer intimacy with direct founder-led improvements.

## 5) Who to Target First (ICP)

Best initial switch targets:
1. Agencies with 10-150 active clients.
2. Multi-branch providers frustrated by price escalation.
3. Providers complaining about implementation drag.
4. Teams needing practical eMAR + visits + care logs in one daily flow.

Avoid first-wave focus on:
1. Very large enterprise procurement cycles unless they are buying dedicated tier.

## 6) Switch Narrative by Competitor

### Against CareLineLive
Use:
1. Lower starting point and predictable package growth.
2. Better onboarding and migration concierge.
3. Founder-level responsiveness and roadmap speed.

### Against Birdie
Use:
1. Lower base cost for smaller agencies.
2. Faster deployment commitment.
3. "No bloat, operationally focused workflows."

### Against Nourish
Use:
1. Simpler all-in-one path for agencies that do not want complex integration overhead.
2. Faster branch-level rollout.
3. Stronger price-to-value for smaller operators.

### Against PASS/everyLIFE and Access
Use:
1. Simpler commercial model and faster onboarding for non-enterprise buyers.
2. Lower total cost to get fully operational.
3. Better hands-on migration support.

## 7) Offer Architecture (So Agencies Actually Move)

Base switch offer:
1. Free data migration for first site.
2. 2-week parallel run.
3. 90-day switch guarantee.
4. Included training bundle.

Commercial structure:
1. Month 1 discounted by 50%.
2. Annual prepay optional, max 10% discount.
3. Overage terms shown up front in quote.

Risk reversal:
1. If go-live milestones missed by us, extend discount window automatically.

## 8) Proof Pack You Need in Every Deal

Every serious buyer should receive:
1. Product capability map (care logs, eMAR, visits, AI summary approvals).
2. Reliability pack (latest suite pass/fail artifacts and incident policy).
3. Security/privacy pack (data handling, AI minimization, audit controls).
4. Migration plan with named owner and exact timeline.
5. ROI calculator output customized to their client/staff numbers.

## 9) ROI Framing for Sales Calls

Use a simple value model:
1. Admin hours saved per coordinator/week.
2. Fewer medication and documentation misses.
3. Faster CQC/audit evidence retrieval.
4. Reduced duplicate entry across disconnected tools.

Position:
1. "Even if we are only slightly cheaper, the operational time saved is the real margin unlock."
